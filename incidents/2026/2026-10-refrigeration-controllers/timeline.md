# Incident Timeline

## 12 October 2026

- CERT-FR published the initial advisory concerning compromises of remote refrigeration controllers.
- News reporting described affected supermarket operations and losses associated with disrupted refrigeration.

## 13 October 2026

- CERT-FR updated the advisory to include additional affected product families.
- Public reporting continued to describe the impact on supermarket refrigeration systems.

## 14 October 2026

- News footage documented supermarkets restocking and workers handling discarded products.

## 15 October 2026 — Research session

- Compared official guidance with news reporting.
- Identified the distinction between confirmed compromise and unconfirmed attack-chain details.
- Derived prevention and detection recommendations.

## Timeline limitations

This timeline records publication and reporting milestones. It is not a complete forensic timeline of the intrusions. The exact sequence of attacker actions remains unconfirmed in the public sources cited here.
